# Typefaces

The Footnotes interface uses three families, all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The licence
texts ship inside the APK at `app/src/main/assets/licenses/`.

| Family | Used for | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Fraunces](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fraunces) | Display and headline type | `ofl/fraunces` in [google/fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts) |
| [Space Grotesk](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Space+Grotesk) | Reading and UI text | `ofl/spacegrotesk` in [google/fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts) |
| [Space Mono](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Space+Mono) | Metadata, stamps and labels | `ofl/spacemono` in [google/fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts) |

Fraunces is used at Bold and Black weights; Space Grotesk at Regular, Medium and Bold; Space Mono at
Regular and Bold. Fraunces Italic is used for a little editorial emphasis, never for body copy.
